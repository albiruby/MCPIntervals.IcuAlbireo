# Prompt Engineering Guide - AI Running Coach

Use these optimized prompts with Claude, Cursor, or Windsurf when connected to TrackLab MCP:

### 1. Weekly Performance Audit
> "Analyze my latest training week using `export_weekly_report`. Evaluate my PMC (CTL/ATL/TSB), Training Intensity Distribution (TID), and check if I'm at risk of injury according to ACWR."

### 2. Threshold Workout Design
> "Call `analyze_threshold` to check my LT1 and LT2 pace. Then use `build_threshold_workout` to generate a 5x1000m cruise interval workout formatted for my Garmin watch."

### 3. Marathon Race Strategy
> "Use `predict_marathon` to project my finish time, and `generate_race_plan` to build a kilometer-by-kilometer negative split pacing chart. Include a fueling schedule from `calculate_race_nutrition`."

### 4. Readiness & Recovery Check
> "Check my readiness score with `get_training_readiness`. Should I execute a hard VO2max session today or take an active recovery run?"
